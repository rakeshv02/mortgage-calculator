Frequently Asked Questions

How is a mortgage payment calculated?
Using the amortisation formula with principal (price minus deposit), monthly interest rate and number of payments.
What is LTV?
Loan-to-Value — the percentage of the property value you're borrowing. Lower LTV usually means better rates.
